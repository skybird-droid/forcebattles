Ability icons. Each ability gets an icon by category (see ABICON at the bottom of js/characters.js):
+1d6, -1d6, +1d6def, -1d6def, stun, taunt, healing, doubleorextraattack, movement (abilities that move you or push an enemy), order66 (Execute Order 66), attack-lightside / attack-darkside (basic attack and plain attack abilities), lightside / darkside (anything else).
To change an ability's icon, change its category in ABICON.
